About the Doctor

Expert Care for Healthy Joints & Autoimmune Conditions

Dr. Henry Collins is an experienced Rheumatologist with over 15 years of expertise in diagnosing and managing arthritis, autoimmune disorders, and inflammatory joint diseases. He is committed to providing individualized treatment plans that improve mobility, relieve pain, and enhance long-term quality of life.

His clinical expertise includes rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, lupus, gout, ankylosing spondylitis, osteoporosis, and connective tissue disorders. He combines advanced diagnostics with evidence-based therapies to help patients effectively manage chronic rheumatic conditions.

Dr. Collins believes that early diagnosis, continuous care, and patient education are essential for preserving joint health and enabling patients to lead active, independent, and fulfilling lives.

Doctor's Philosophy

"Managing rheumatic diseases requires compassion, precision, and long-term partnership. My goal is to help every patient move comfortably and live life to the fullest."
