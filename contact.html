Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions? We've Got Answers

Find answers to some of the most common questions about appointments, consultations, and our medical services.

How do I book an appointment? +

You can easily book an appointment online through our website, call our reception desk, or visit the hospital for in-person scheduling.

What are your consultation hours? +

General consultations are available Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Emergency services operate 24/7.

What documents should I bring for my consultation? +

Please bring a valid ID, previous medical records, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and your health insurance details if applicable.

Do you offer online consultations? +

Yes. Selected specialists provide secure online consultations for follow-up visits and non-emergency medical advice.

How can I reschedule or cancel my appointment? +

You can reschedule or cancel your appointment by contacting our support team or managing your booking through our online appointment portal.

Do you provide emergency medical services? +

Yes. Our emergency department is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week to provide immediate medical care.

What should I do in case of a medical emergency? +

For medical emergencies, immediately contact our 24/7 emergency department or call our emergency helpline for prompt assistance.

Which insurance plans do you accept? +

We work with a wide range of insurance providers. Please contact our support team to verify your specific insurance coverage.
